## Key takeaways
- You can start with photos on WhatsApp, before any travel.
- Our team reviews your photos and confirms the plan.
- Your plan should be in writing: method, hairline design, what is included and the price.
- Aftercare continues after you go home, with photo reviews.

## Step 1: send your photos
Send clear photos in daylight: front, top, back and both sides of your head. Add a short note about how long you have lost hair, past treatments and any health conditions or medicines.

## Step 2: expert review
Our team studies your donor area, the pattern of your hair loss and what you want to achieve. If you are not a good candidate, a responsible clinic tells you so.

## Step 3: your written plan
The plan describes the suggested method, the hairline design, what the price includes and what is not included. Compare plans on the same basis: ask what is included in each one.

## Step 4: treatment and aftercare
Treatment takes place in Istanbul. You then receive a written aftercare plan and follow-up photo reviews, so you can ask questions after you travel home.

## Warning signs
- A price or a graft number promised without anyone reviewing your hair.
- Pressure to decide quickly, or a discount that expires today.
- No clear answer about who plans the hairline and who performs the treatment.
- No written plan or aftercare information.

## Sources
- [International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery (ISHRS)](https://ishrs.org/)
- [MedlinePlus: Hair loss](https://medlineplus.gov/hairloss.html)

В плане описаны предлагаемый метод, дизайн линии роста волос, что входит в цену и что не входит. Сравнивайте планы на одной основе: спрашивайте, что включено в каждый.

Операция проходит в Стамбуле. После неё вы получаете письменный план ухода и контрольные проверки по фото, поэтому можно задавать вопросы и после возвращения домой.

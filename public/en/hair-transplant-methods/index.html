Hair transplant methods: Sapphire FUE, DHI, beard and eyebrow

Updated 2026-11-13

Key takeaways

  • FUE and DHI both move your own hair follicles from a donor area to the thinning area.
  • The best method depends on your hair loss, donor area and goals, and your clinic team decides it after reviewing your photos.
  • Be careful with claims that one method is always better. Ask for the reasoning in writing.

Sapphire FUE

In FUE, individual follicular units are taken one by one from the donor area, usually the back and sides of the head. Small channels are then opened in the recipient area and the grafts are placed in them. Sapphire FUE uses sapphire-tipped blades to open those channels.

DHI

DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) uses a pen-like implanter that holds each graft and places it directly into the skin. The extraction step is similar to FUE.

Beard and eyebrow transplant

The principle is the same: hair follicles, usually from the scalp, are placed where beard or eyebrow hair is thin or missing. The direction and angle of the hairs need careful planning so that the result looks natural.

Which method suits you?

It depends on how much hair you have lost, the quality of your donor area, the shape of the hairline you want and how stable your hair loss is. your clinic team looks at these points on your photos and in the consultation.

What to ask a clinic

  • Who plans the hairline and who performs each step?
  • Why is this method suitable for my hair, and what are the alternatives?
  • How many sessions might I need, and what happens if I keep losing hair?
  • What is included in the written price, and what is aftercare?

Frequently asked questions

Is FUE or DHI better?

Neither is better for everyone. The choice depends on your hair loss, donor area and goals, and your clinic team explains the reasons.

Can I have a beard or eyebrow transplant?

Often yes, if you have enough donor hair. Our team checks this on your photos.

Do I need a second session?

Some patients do, for example if hair loss continues. Our team can tell you what is likely in your case.

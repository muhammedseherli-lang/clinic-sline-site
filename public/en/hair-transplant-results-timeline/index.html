Hair transplant results: what to expect from day 1 to month 12

Updated 2026-11-13

Key takeaways

  • Redness, swelling and small scabs are common in the first days.
  • Transplanted hairs usually shed in the first weeks, then regrow.
  • Clear results appear around 6 months, and the final result around 12 months.
  • Follow your clinic team's written plan for washing, sport and sun protection.

The first days

Expect redness, mild swelling and small scabs in the treated area. The clinic team shows you how to wash your scalp, and you receive a written aftercare plan.

Weeks 2 to 4

The transplanted hairs usually shed. This is a normal part of the process, because the follicles stay in place and prepare to grow new hair.

Months 3 to 6

New hair starts to grow, often thin at first. It thickens gradually.

Months 6 to 12

Density and texture keep improving. Most patients see a clear result at around 6 months and the final result at around 12 months.

Aftercare

your clinic team gives the exact timing for your case. In general: follow the wash guide, protect the scalp from the sun, and wait for your clinic team's approval before sport or heavy exercise.

Sources

Frequently asked questions

Is shedding after a transplant normal?

Yes. Transplanted hairs usually shed in the first weeks and regrow over the following months.

When will I see the final result?

Most patients see clear results at around 6 months and the final result at around 12 months.

When can I return to work or sport?

It depends on your case and your clinic team's advice. Ask for the exact timing in your written aftercare plan.

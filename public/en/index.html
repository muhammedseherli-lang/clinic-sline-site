Recovery timeline

What most patients can expect after a hair transplant, from the first days to month 12.

  2. Weeks 2 to 4Transplanted hairs shed
  3. Months 3 to 6New hair starts to grow

Frequently asked questions

Which photos should I send?

Front, top, back and both sides of your head, in daylight and without a hat.

Do I need to travel before I get a plan?

No. You can start with photos on WhatsApp. Our team confirms the final plan.

When will I see the result?

Hairs usually shed in the first weeks and regrow. Most patients see a clear result at around 6 months and the final result at around 12 months.

Am I a suitable candidate?

It depends on your donor area, hair quality and how stable your hair loss is. Our team checks this on your photos.
